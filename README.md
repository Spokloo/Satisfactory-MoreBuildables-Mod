---

Get more buildables such as foundations or walls to make your factory look better!\
This is for all of you builders that are missing just the right buildable to fit somewhere you need.\
This mod aims to keep the vanilla feel to the experience and therefore adds buildables that could (should) be in vanilla.

---

### Features

Every buildable can be unlocked directly in the AWESOME Shop.

#### Foundations

Each foundation is available in all variants (1m, 2m, 4m) and in all materials.

- Quarter Foundations
- Inner Corner Ramps
- Outer Corner Ramps
- Inverted Inner Corner Ramps
- Inverted Outer Corner Ramps

### Known bugs

- [#9](https://github.com/Spokloo/Satisfactory-MoreBuildables-Mod/issues/9): Inverted Inner/Outer Corner Ramps don't show the FICSIT material when it is the first unlocked foundation within its subcategory.
